# ELARA -- ordering the amplifier shield (JLCCNC)

The antenna-amplifier shield (frame, tray and lid, `mechanical/README.md` section 1)
can be ordered from JLCCNC, JLCPCB's machining service: CNC milling for the bars
and the tray, the sheet-metal service for the lid. Everything to upload is in
`mechanical/jlccnc/`, generated by `mechanical/jlccnc.py` from the same parameters
as the 3D models. `tools/check_all.sh` probes every feature in the drawings against
its STEP solid, so the drawings cannot drift from the models.

Each part is one STEP file (the 3D model, origin at a corner) plus one PDF drawing
(views, a table of every feature, the notes). The tapped holes are modelled at
tap-drill size, so **the PDF must be uploaded too**: without it the threads are
not cut.

64 tapped M3 holes in all (48 vertical in the bar faces, 16 in the bar ends for the
corner and tee joints); the tray and the lid have plain holes only. The parts'
names in the order must match the STEP file names.

## Options to set

| Option | Bars and tray (CNC milling) | Lid (sheet metal) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Material | Aluminium 6061 (T6) | Aluminium 5052, 2 mm |
| Surface finish | **Bead blasting**, or as machined | As cut, or bead blasting |
| Anodising | **No** (see below) | **No** |
| Threads | Yes: upload the part's PDF as its 2D / thread drawing | none |
| Tolerance | Standard (ISO 2768-m); the tighter ones are on the drawings | Standard |
| Quantity | as in the table, times the number of shields | 1 per shield |

**Never anodise.** Anodising is an insulator: the bars and the tray clamp the PCB's
bare ground strips between them, and the lid closes the shield on the bar tops.
Every contact face must conduct. A conductive chromate (chemical film, e.g. Alodine
1200 / SurTec 650) is welcome if offered; otherwise do it locally, or leave the parts
bead-blasted and bare (they live inside the sealed outdoor box).

## Note to paste into the order ("Remark" field)

```text
Electronics shield parts. Please cut ALL threads shown in the PDF drawings
(M3x0.5-6H, thread and drill depths as listed in each table). NO anodising:
the faces must stay electrically conductive (bead blast or as machined;
conductive chromate/chemical film is fine). The 52 mm height of the four bar
parts must match within +/-0.05 and the contact faces (top and bottom of the
bars, top of the tray) must be flat within 0.05 mm. Thank you.
```

## Not from JLCCNC

- **PTFE feed-through terminals (A6, 2 pieces + spares):** Keystone 11157 from DigiKey
  (36-11157-ND) or Mouser: PTFE-insulated press-fit pin-to-pin feed-throughs for a
  3.45 mm hole in a 1.6 mm panel. The tray drawing (SH-05) has the press-fit holes
  in Ø8 spot-faces that thin the 2 mm floor to 1.6 mm there. Press-fits are
  single-use, so buy two or three spares.
  - Look-alikes to avoid: "PTFE flanged bushings" from bearing makers (SKF PCMF,
    Isostatic FM...) are PTFE-lined **steel** sleeves and conduct.
  - Until 2026-11-01 the design used a lathe-turned PTFE bush (Ø18 flange, Ø10
    spigot, Ø1.2 bore) in a Ø10 hole. Nobody sells it ready-made; it would need the
    tray holes opened to Ø10.
- **Screws (A7-A9):** stainless button and socket heads from `bom/mechanical_bom.csv`.
- **PSU box and outdoor parts:** not in this package (`mechanical/README.md`).

## When the parts arrive

1. Check every thread with an M3 screw (or a 6H plug gauge); a missed thread is the
   usual slip.
2. Stand the four bar parts on a flat surface: the tops must be level (52 +/-0.05).
3. Dry-fit the frame on the bare PCB (no parts) before assembling: the walls sit
   only on the 7 mm ground strips, and every PCB hole lines up with a tap.
4. Press one Keystone 11157 into a spot-faced hole: it must seat flush on the
   spot-face and not turn by hand.
5. With a multimeter, measure from a bar's bottom face to the tray's top face through
   a clamped bare PCB strip: well under 1 ohm. Higher means an insulating finish.
